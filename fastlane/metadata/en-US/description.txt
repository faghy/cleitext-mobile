Cleitext keeps your site with you. Add your Cleitext sites, sign in with your account and stay on top of everything from your phone.

WRITE WHEN THE IDEA COMES
A title, some text and a few photos taken on the spot: save a draft or publish right away. Bold, italic, headings and lists are there when you need them, and you can polish everything later on your computer.

COMMENTS IN ONE TAP
Approve, reply, mark as spam or trash the comments waiting for you, wherever you are.

MESSAGES AND ORDERS
Read the messages sent through your site's forms and answer by email or phone in one tap. If your site has a shop, follow new orders, see their details and change their status when you ship them.

PHOTOS AND MEDIA
Snap a photo and upload it to your site's library in seconds.

NOTIFICATIONS YOU CHOOSE
An alert when a comment waits for approval, a message arrives or an order comes in. Choose site by site what to receive.

MANY SITES, ONE APP
Switch between your sites with a tap. For everything else there is "Open the full admin".

THEMES AND PLUGINS FOR YOUR SITE
The Shop section lists Cleitext's paid themes and plugins with clear prices. The purchase is completed on cleitext.com and the licence is used on your website. You can also create your Cleitext account.

RESPECT FOR YOUR DATA
No ads, no tracking. The app talks only to the sites you add; your sign-in is stored securely on the phone and you can remove it at any time.

Cleitext is an open-source CMS, and this app is free software too.
