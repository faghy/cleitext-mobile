SIGN-IN
Cleitext is an app for people who run websites made with Cleitext, an open-source CMS. To use it you add your site and sign in with your account on that site.
1. Tap "Aggiungi sito" / "Add a site".
2. Type demo.cleitext.com and tap "Continue".
3. The site's sign-in page opens in the browser: sign in with the demo username and password given in the App Review Information, then tap "Allow".
4. You are back in the app with the site's dashboard: comments to approve, form messages, shop orders, Write (posts with photos), Media and Chriss, the site's assistant (round button at the bottom right).

SHOP TAB
The Shop tab lists licences for Cleitext website themes and plugins. They are software licences installed and used on the customer's own website (a service consumed outside the app); nothing in the app is unlocked or changed by a purchase. Checkout happens on cleitext.com in the browser. Creating a Cleitext account is optional; the account can be deleted from the app (More > Cleitext account > Delete account).

NOTIFICATIONS AND DATA
Notifications are sent by the user's own site. The app has no analytics, ads or tracking.
